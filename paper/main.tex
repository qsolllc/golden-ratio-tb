\begin{abstract}
We present exact diagonalization of the Aubry-Andre model with golden ratio $\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$ on Fibonacci lengths $L=13,21,34,55$ on a mobile device. We demonstrate metal-insulator transition at $\lambda_c=2t$ via (i) inverse participation ratio IPR scaling $\sim1/L$ extended vs const localized, (ii) level spacing ratio $\langle r\rangle$ from $0.7242$ GOE-like to $0.3529$ Poisson. All results hash-committed with SHA3-512 and RFC8785 JCS, with 46-stitch POCATELLO chain tracking convergence. Repo: github.com/qsolllc/golden-ratio-tb
\end{abstract}
